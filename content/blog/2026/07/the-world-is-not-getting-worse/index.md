It seems as though "anything that sticks a thorn into the side of our tech overlords is good" is just a universally accepted worldview these days.

Maybe I'm too sheltered or something, but I just don't see things that way. I have no idea what these people are witnessing that is turning them into such doomers. "Big Tech" isn't the one who's lobbying for access to your state ID. They don't *want* your state ID. They lobby specifically *against* being required to access your state ID. Why would Jeff Bezos *want* your state ID??? What would he do with that? He doesn't want your DOB, he wants your credit card.

It's a commonly accepted belief that our "Tech Overlords" are bad. Why? idk



Logical debate aside, I simply don't share the emotional response. I go to Amazon, I click stuff, it arrives quickly. That rules. I go to some website where I want to buy something neat and Shopify makes the transaction smooth and easy and trustworthy. Awesome. I go on Reddit and talk to people from around the world and see the news of the day. Well, some drawbacks, but still, cool. If I ever get cancer, I can have genomic testing done with highly portable results between hospitals via interoperability that would have been really hard a decade ago. I visit Dubuque and ChatGPT helps me lay out an itin for the weekend. And on and on and on. Our tech overlords sell me a bunch of products that I like and I buy them of my free will. I sincerely don't share whatever the gut feeling is that makes other people despise the rich guys in tech.
